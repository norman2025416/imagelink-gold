AI AGENT

The agent works when you press a button. It uses your own Claude API key and credit, searches the web, and shows its sources. It suggests; you decide. It cannot open accounts, spend money or post for you.

1. FIND PROGRAMS

Searches for affiliate programs in a category that accept people in your country.

2. FIND PRODUCTS

Searches current best-seller lists and picks products worth promoting, with the page it found each on.

3. REVIEW MY RESULTS

Reads your sales, codes and adverts and tells you what to push, fix or drop. No web search.
